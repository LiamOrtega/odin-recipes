Empanadas: there is No better food.

Home

A delicious little individual pastry made from a folded and sealed dough with a braided edge, filled with a juicy stew of meat, onions, and peppers. It can be enjoyed fried or baked.

I dare you to find a better meal than this traditional Argentinian dish that's over 150 years old. Spoiler alert: you won't find one. Tacos don't even come close, and with the recipe I'm about to share, you'll have the best ones right in your hands and within easy reach of your mouth.

Prepare everything, invite your friends and let yourself go because with this dish you're going to have an unforgettable day.

Ingredients:

  • All-Purpose Flour
  • Unsalted Butter
  • Eggs
  • Water or Milk
  • Salt
  • Beef
  • Onion
  • Garlic

    • And the secrets...

  • Cumin
  • Paprika
  • Chili Powder
  • Bell Pepper

Steps to make the best empanada:

  1. Make the Dough: Mixing flour and salt, rubbing in cold butter until crumbly, and stirring in a whisked egg and water to form a dough that you will knead and chill for 30 minutes.
  2. The Filling: While it chills, saute onion, garlic, and bell pepper, brown the ground beef with cumin, paprika, chili powder, salt, and pepper, and then simmer the mixture with beef stock before letting it cool completely.
  3. Assembly: roll the dough to a one-eighth-inch thickness, cut out five-inch circles, place two tablespoons of the cooled filling onto each disc, and fold them into half-moons, pressing the edges with a fork to seal.
  4. Cooking: Finally, arrange the empanadas on a baking sheet, brush the tops with a beaten egg wash, and bake at 200°C for 20 to 25 minutes until golden brown.
  5. Savor: Relax, it was difficult but you did it. Call your closest companion and enjoy the best dish of all time.
